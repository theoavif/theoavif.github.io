About

French artist born in Dijon, based in Barcelona for over eight years. Theo Avif works across analogue photography, collage and painting to explore memory, identity and our relationship to the real in an increasingly digital world.

Since 2022, he has developed a series of projects blending exhibition and experience — from fine art architecture photography to mixed media collage and acrylic painting. Each work is available as an original piece or limited fine art print. Available in Barcelona, Paris, Dijon and Marseille.
